Pip Hunt

Pip is a tiny, very shy gnome who hides behind window frames, door edges and curtains in the room around you. Tap him before the clock runs out.

  • 1Stand in one spot. Hold the phone upright and turn a full circle so Pip can learn the room.
  • 2Look around and tap Pip when you spot him peeking out. Every catch scores, and fast catches score more.
  • 3Miss, and he gets scared and runs to a new spot. Walk away from your spot and you are disqualified.

The camera only works when this page is opened over https. Use the demo room here, or host the file (see the note that came with it).
